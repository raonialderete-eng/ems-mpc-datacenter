\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

I am pleased to resubmit the manuscript entitled

\begin{center}
\textit{Predictive Energy Management of an Aggregated AI/HPC Data-Center Load with BESS:\\
When Forecast-Aware Control Suffices and When MPC Adds Value}
\end{center}

as a Research Article in \textit{IEEE Access}, following the decision to reject the previous version in present form with encouragement to resubmit.

This work presents an applied, comparative energy management framework for AI/HPC data centers with BESS providing UPS support. The scientific question is when forecast-aware simple control already removes converter-limited transients, and when multi-step QP still adds value. Six controllers share the same plant and cases: a reactive heuristic, a foresight-enabled heuristic, a myopic QP, a feedforward-plus-feedback inversion law, a deterministic MPC-QP (V5), and a three-scenario MPC with a shared command sequence.

The study focuses on AI-typical demand steps and source-limit saturation. Under nominal load variation both V5 and FF+FB drive filtered unserved-power peaks below 1 kW, so multi-step QP is sufficient but not uniquely necessary. Under source loss, V5 reaches the physical bound 350 kW; peak mitigation is not energy autonomy. A reduced-horizon copy of the same MPC, solved with OSQP on a Cortex-A9, closed the loop on the aggregated MATLAB plant; recorded island peaks are MPC+fallback. Solver times on the board did not exceed 1 s; end-to-end 1 Hz deadline compliance was not assessed.

The experimental pack \texttt{07\_Experimental\_Pack.zip} remains on the GitHub release of tag \texttt{v1.0.4-ieee-access-rev} (job MATs, \texttt{dataset\_v1}, B-group CSVs, audited MATLAB). The addendum \texttt{08\_Experimental\_Addendum.zip} on tag \texttt{v1.0.5-ieee-access-rev} holds OSQP/DE10 HIL CSVs and diagnostic CSVs.
